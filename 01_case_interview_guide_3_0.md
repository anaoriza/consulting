## BAGIAN 1 — TEORI 5 TIPE KASUS

### 1. Profitability Case
- **Definisi:** analisis sumber revenue & cost lalu cari cara naikkan profit.
- **3 sub-tipe:**
  - **Profit problem** — "PepsiCo profit turun, analisis & perbaiki" (paling straightforward).
  - **Loss problem** — "Swiggy rugi 3 tahun" → cari kenapa rugi & beri rekomendasi.
  - **Margin problem** — "Myntra margin menyempit" → cari kenapa margin mengetat.
- **Preliminary questions:** Sejak kapan & seberapa besar? Di mana klien beroperasi? Apakah demand pasar keseluruhan turun / industry-wide? Apakah semua toko/produk kena? Apakah pasar menyusut? Apakah kompetitor menekan harga? Ada lonjakan biaya mendadak?
- **Framework:** Profitability framework, Quantitative vs Qualitative, Value Chain Analysis, Bull's Eye Framework.
- **Cheatsheet Profitability (pohon):**
  - Profit = Revenue − Costs.
  - Revenue = Quantity Sold × Price per Unit; alternatif: Total Customers × Revenue per Customer; Revenue per customer = Order Frequency × Average Order Value (AOV).
  - Revenue (demand side) → 4P (Product, Price, Place, Promotion), Customer Service, Pricing Issues.
  - Costs = Fixed + Variable (+ Interest/Cost of Capital). Juga Unit Economics & Profit Margins.
  - Supply side → Value chain produsen: Procurement → Production → Distribution → Marketing.
  - **Fixed costs contoh:** Leases, Depreciation, Licensing fees, Salaries, Taxes, Amortisation, Bank fees, Mortgages.
  - **Variable / misc costs contoh:** Customer Acquisition Cost (CAC), Labour wages, Raw materials, Shipping & freight, Legal fees, Packaging, Utilities, Inventory & warehousing, Outsourcing fees, Manufacturing costs.

### 2. Growth Case
- Fokus pada setengah pertama persamaan profit: **Revenue = Price × Quantity**. Contoh: Coca-Cola flat growth, cari cara tumbuh.
- **Organic growth** = pertumbuhan internal dari operasi (lebih sustainable): via revenue source lama atau baru.
- **Inorganic growth** = beli bisnis lain/buka lokasi baru: Acquisitions, Joint Ventures, Partnerships, M&A.
- **Preliminary questions:** Product mix klien? Sudah berapa lama masalah? Ada perubahan besar selama periode itu? Seluruh perusahaan atau produk/geografi tertentu? Apa channel distribusi & apakah penurunan merata di semua channel? Industry-wide atau company-specific? Dana tersedia untuk growth?
- **Framework:** BCG Matrix, PESTEL, Needscope Analysis, Ansoff Matrix, Value Disciplines Framework.
- **Cheatsheet Growth:** Growth = Revenue Growth + Cost Reduction.
  - Revenue growth: Organic (Number of customers × Revenue per customer; Increase market size: domestic/international; New products; New customer segments; Diversify revenue stream; Product diversification) vs Inorganic (JV, M&A).
  - Cost reduction: Fixed (Depreciation, Amortisation, Salaries, Taxes, Land & building, Rental/lease, Plant & machinery, Fixed charges & fees) vs Variable (Logistics, Packaging, Distribution, Marketing, Post services, Wages, Raw material, Warehousing).
  - Market analysis: strategi masuk pasar (sifat pasar, regulasi geografi target, entry barriers).
  - **Strategi keluar pasar (exit):** jual ke Private Equity; salvage aset & tutup operasi; "dry the company out" (peras kas hingga habis); IPO.

### 3. M&A Case
- Contoh: Walmart akuisisi platform online UMKM? McDonald's merger dengan Pizza Hut? Unstop akuisisi Internshala? PE akuisisi rantai tattoo parlor?
- **Dua tipe:** (a) Company acquiring/merging dengan company lain (sering berbentuk GTM strategy — business unit yang mau ekspansi offering / launch unit baru); (b) PE firm mengakuisisi company (biasanya diarahkan ke growing revenue atau expanding margin via price elasticity / harga + perubahan cost).
- **Preliminary questions:** Industri apa? Market size & growth? Kompetitor kunci? Fokus pasar high-volume/low-margin atau low-volume/high-margin? Ancaman? Segmen pelanggan kunci? Barriers to entry?
- **Framework:** McKinsey M&A Framework, McKinsey 7S.
- **Cheatsheet M&A — "Net benefit of the merger/acquisition":**
  - **Financial:** Value addition / Synergies (Financial, Operational, Revenue, Costs) vs Costs (Acquisition price, Integration costs, Company valuation).
  - **Non-financial:** Acquirer fit, Cultural fit, Strategy fit, Organizational fit; External risks (PESTEL).
  - **Exit / PMI strategy** (post-merger integration): When, How, Cost; Redesign — Culture, Systems, Strategy, Structure, Navigation. (Catatan: guide salah menulis PMI = "Purchasing Managers Index"; dalam konteks M&A PMI = Post-Merger Integration.)
  - **Type of deal:** Horizontal, Vertical integration, Diversification, Market entry.

### 4. Market Entry Case
- Evaluasi peluang growth spesifik: go/no-go; uji kelayakan ekonomi & operasional.
- **Sub-tipe:** Geographic expansion (Chipotle ke Eropa?), Product line expansion (TVS luncurkan e-scooter?), Product segment (Apple luncurkan bohlam?), General growth strategy tersamar ("klien di pasar jenuh, harus apa?").
- **Preliminary:** Geografi yang dilayani? Tujuan ekspansi? Kompetitor eksisting? Substitusi/potential entrants? Keunggulan lokasi yang hemat biaya? Faktor makro/sosial/geopolitik?
- **Framework:** Blue Ocean, Cost-Benefit Analysis, Porter's Five Forces, 4P/7P.
- **Cheatsheet Market Entry:** 3 cabang — **Financial Attractiveness**, **Operational Feasibility**, **Risks**.
  - Financial: Market growth rate, competitive landscape, **Expected Profits = (Market size in units × % Market share × Profit/unit) − Fixed costs**. Profit/unit = Selling price/unit − Variable cost/unit. Bila ditanya revenue tahun pertama → pakai selling price/unit, bukan profit/unit. Market size (units) sering diminta via guesstimate.
  - **Go-to-Market:** STP — Segmentation (Geographic, Demographic, Behavioral), Targeting (segment size, segment growth, segment selection), Positioning; lalu 4P.

### 5. Pricing Case
- Menentukan harga produk/jasa (mis. iPhone baru).
- **3 strategi:**
  - **Value-based** → batas atas (maximum price): WTP maksimal dibanding substitusi kompetitor untuk tingkat kepuasan sama. Faktor: price of substitutes, utility to customer, necessity vs luxury.
  - **Cost-based** → batas bawah (minimum price): variable cost/unit + allocated fixed cost/unit (pakai production capacity atau market demand — *whichever is lower*).
  - **Competitor-based** → rentang: price range kompetitor, perbedaan produk, variasi dari harga kompetitor.
- **Preliminary:** Produk apa & posisi di pasar? Tujuan utama perusahaan? Diferensiasi vs kompetitor? Alternatif/substitusi? Tahap product lifecycle? Supply & demand predictable?
- **Framework:** Pricing strategies, Revenue Stream Matrix, Customer Journey Map, 5Cs of Pricing.
- Demand-side factors: differentiating factors, consumer characteristics, price benchmarking, WTP, **Total Cost of Ownership**, semua cara penggunaan produk, kelebihan/kekurangan, seberapa beda dari produk lain.

6. **Kolaboratif:** cek arah dengan interviewer, minta waktu menyusun struktur, terima penolakan dan pivot.

## BAGIAN 4 — GUESSTIMATES

### Teori
- **Guesstimate** = estimasi angka dengan info terbatas memakai tebakan + asumsi masuk akal.
- **Demand-side (consumer-centric):** asumsikan tanpa batasan supply; mulai dari total accessible population → filter (needs, preferences, purchasing power, niche) → angka akhir. Contoh: market size produk bayi India, speaker terjual/tahun.
- **Supply-side (producer-centric):** dipakai saat ada batasan supply (kapasitas produksi tetap, waktu kirim, regulasi/transportasi). Estimasi via kapasitas unit distribusi × utilisasi × durasi; fokus mencari **bottleneck**. Contoh: jumlah penerbangan IGI per hari, pendapatan tol bulanan Delhi NCR.
- **Struktur demand-side:** Relevant population → Filter 1 (x+y+z=100%) → Filter 2 (a+b+c=100%) → Filter 3 (m+n=100%) → #users × consumption pattern = total. **3–6 filter** tergantung kasus. Parameter: urbanisasi, gender, literasi, penetrasi internet/mobile, income divide, usia, employment (formal/informal), seasonality, household size, lifestyle.
- **Struktur supply-side:** Market size = #distribution units × sales per unit × price. Sales per unit = Σ(periode lean/medium/peak) jam × max capacity × utilisation rate. Jumlah unit distribusi via 3 cara: (a) total serviceable area ÷ area per unit (luas Delhi ÷ area per toll booth); (b) key players × unit yang dimiliki (distributor Rolex × outlet); (c) total addressable population ÷ populasi dilayani per unit (penduduk Delhi ÷ orang per rumah sakit). Skala unit: small/medium/large.
- **Contoh Bistro 57:** buka 12 jam = lean 3 jam (30%), medium 5 jam (60%), peak 4 jam (100%); kapasitas 60/jam → lean 3 × 60 × 30% = 54 dst.
- **Contoh Baby care:** 1,4 M penduduk, harapan hidup 60 → usia 0–2 = 1,4 M × 2/60 ≈ 46 jt; urban 40%/rural 60%; rich 20%/middle 30%/poor 50%; konversi 80–100%; konsumsi 650 g/minggu.

### Cheatsheet angka India (asumsi standar)
- Populasi 1,40 M; urban 40% / rural 60%; gender 55% L / 45% P.
- Usia: 0–18 = 30%, 19–25 = 10%, 26–40 = 30%, 41–60 = 20%, 60+ = 10% (versi tabel).
- Income: BPL 25%, lower middle 50%, upper middle 15%, upper 10%.
- Literasi 77% (urban 85%, rural 70%); internet 50% (urban 55%, rural 45%); smartphone 55%; listrik 99%; luas Delhi 1.500 km².
- Pengeluaran: food 30%, housing 30%, travel 10%, healthcare 10%, education 10%, misc 10%.
- Sektor GDP: services 55%, industry 30%, agri 15% (kurang lebih).

### Prinsip guesstimate yang dipelajari
1. **Clarify scope**: unit (kuantitas vs nilai), periode, geografi, definisi ("aktif", "wrongful"), segmen yang dimasukkan (komersial? turis? pirated?).
2. Pilih **demand vs supply** (supply untuk hal fisik dengan kapasitas: tol, penerbangan, desa, rickshaw).
3. Segmentasi **MECE** & jumlah persen = 100% per filter; jelaskan alasan tiap eliminasi.
4. Gunakan **rata-rata tertimbang** untuk konsumsi heterogen (light/medium/heavy).
5. Bagi dengan **lifespan / replacement cycle** untuk barang tahan lama.
6. **Sanity check** terhadap angka pembanding (total pasar, % populasi).
7. Bulatkan angka agar mudah dihitung (28,8 jt → 30 jt).
8. Untuk peak/lean: pisahkan periode × utilisasi.

## GLOSSARY (dari guide)
- **Benchmarking:** membandingkan kinerja dengan standar/praktik terbaik (mis. vs market leader).
- **B2B / B2C:** transaksi antar-bisnis / bisnis ke konsumen akhir.
- **Balanced Scorecard:** mengukur kinerja (finansial, nilai pelanggan, bisnis) via tujuan terukur; metrik: cash flow, likuiditas, improvement index, breakeven.
- **Bottleneck:** titik kemacetan yang menghambat proses.
- **Bottom-up / Top-down:** dari detail ke gambaran besar / dari gambaran besar ke detail.
- **Competitive advantage:** kekuatan unik untuk mengungguli pesaing.
- **Break-even point:** kuantitas di mana total revenue = total cost.
- **CAGR:** compound annual growth rate.
- **Competitor-/Cost-/Value-based pricing.**
- **Core competencies:** hal terbatas yang secara inheren dikuasai perusahaan.
- **GTM:** taktik membawa produk ke pasar (marketing, sales, distribusi, support).
- **Cost/Revenue drivers;** **Horizontal integration** (akuisisi di level value chain yang sama); **Vertical integration** (memiliki pemasok hulu/pembeli hilir).
- **Inbound/Outbound logistics.**
- **Cross-selling / Upselling.**
- **KPI.**
- **Outsourcing; Market share; Stand-alone value** (nilai intrinsik tanpa sinergi); **Synergy** (gabungan > jumlah bagian, terutama M&A).
- **MECE:** Mutually Exclusive, Collectively Exhaustive.
- **Paradigm shift; Portfolio; Value chain.**
